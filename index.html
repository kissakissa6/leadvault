How it works

  1. Send us your website and tell us who your best customers are. That’s all we need to start.
  2. We send back a few companies from your market, with the person to contact at each and why they fit. This costs you nothing.
  3. If it looks right, order a list. We research each company, find the decision-maker and check their work email. You get the file within 48 hours.

What you get

Every row is a company you could call tomorrow: its name, country, region and line of business, the person who decides there by name and job title, and one sentence on why they’d buy, so you know how to start the conversation.

We check each work email with the company’s mail server and note the result in the list. Some servers accept any address, so a few can’t be fully confirmed, and those are marked.

The file comes as Excel and as a HubSpot CSV. Open it in any spreadsheet or import it straight into HubSpot.

Contact details come from companies’ own websites and official business registers, never from data brokers. How we handle data

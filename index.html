How it works

  1. Send us your website and tell us who your best customers are. That’s all we need to start.
  2. We send back a few companies from your market, with the person to contact at each and why they fit. This costs you nothing.
  3. If it looks right, order a list. We research each company, find the decision-maker and check their work email. You get the file within 48 hours.

What you get

Every company on your list is ready to call. You get its name, country, region and line of business, the person who decides there by name and job title, and one sentence on why they’d buy, so you know how to start the conversation.

We check each work email with the company’s mail server. If the server accepts any address, we can’t fully confirm the email, so we mark it “Likely”.

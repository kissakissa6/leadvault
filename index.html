Method

How a list is built

The same four steps produced Exhibit 1.

  1. You send your website

    Or you reply to the email we sent you, which means we have already read it.

  2. We work out who buys from you

    From what your site says you make and who you serve, we pick the kinds of company that buy it, then draw them from the register for your region.

  3. We find the people who decide

    On each company’s own website, keeping the website, and often the exact page, each person came from. Where a strong buying signal exists, such as a registry change or a public contract won, we add it.

  4. You see a sample first

    Three to five of your own likely buyers, free. If it is useful, you order a list and get it within 48 hours as Excel and HubSpot CSV.
